# Accessing Vahana Portal
To access the Vahana platform:
1. Open and Internet browser.
2.	In the address box, enter the URL of Vahana platform and then press **Enter** to display the login page.
3. On the login page, enter the values as follows:
   * In the **Email ID** box, enter your registered email ID as user ID.
   * In the **Password** box, enter the password of your Vahana user account.
4.	After you enter the email id and password, the **Sign In** link becomes active.
5. Click the **Sign** In link to display home page of the Vahana cloud.
